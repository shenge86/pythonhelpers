# Chapter 6: The Game

Kara sat at the edge of her bed pondering on whether to sleep or start the game. She just called it the game since there was only one game she actively played. In a world of science and technology, perhaps there is still an allure to magic that can never be truly satisfied. The closest to magic that she knew was in videogames. In particular, a role-playing game where where the main character can level up.
