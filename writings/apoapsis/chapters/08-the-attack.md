# Chapter 8: The Attack [Gamma]

They came in the middle of the night. Of course, on the spaceship there was no such thing as the usual day and night but they still followed an artificial dimming of lights at the appropriate time to match what they would have experienced on Earth.

Most of the crew afterwards described them as nightmares. There were two that especially stood out. They both appeared to the scientists in the science lab. One was Dennis Roberts and the other was Shen's who was visiting the science lab.
